Your data stays on your computer

Pulse Trace runs entirely in your browser. When you open a CSV, it is read on your own machine and is never uploaded to frantictaco.ca or anywhere else. Nothing is kept on the website, and closing the tab clears it.

Want to use it offline? Save this page to your computer (Ctrl+S, or Cmd+S on a Mac) and open the saved file any time.
